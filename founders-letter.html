a letter from our founder

you know the feeling.

application 784. 3 rounds in, then silence. told you need experience to get experience.

a rejection email at 1:30 in the morning. nobody was awake. nobody read it.

and when nobody tells you what was missing, you start wondering if you were missing something. that part stays with you.

i've spent the last 3 years on the other side of that wall.

i led product at outer circle, one of the biggest degree apprenticeship communities in the UK, with over 8,500 members. i've mentored more than 100 people into roles.

and i co-founded OCF, a founder residency across oxford, cambridge and london, where i've watched startups get built up close. along the way i've worked with and advised founders at every stage, from a first MVP to companies that have raised millions.

from that seat you see both sides. students sending 200 applications into silence. founders drowning in 1,000 applications a role, unable to tell who can deliver.

the talent was there. the opportunities were there. what was missing was a way to prove it.

here's why.

you can't gauge a person from what they say about themselves anymore. AI has made faking competence essentially free. a CV, a cover letter, even a take home can be generated in minutes.

what it hasn't done is make it any easier to know who did the work, what they owned, or how they made the calls.

so the system fights slop with filters. keyword ranking. 7 second skims. rejection emails sent while everyone's asleep.

it's already in the courts. the biggest AI hiring case in america has over a billion rejected applications on the record before a human ever looked.

so what's left?

the only signals worth trusting are the ones that survive real scrutiny. visible work, judged by a trained eye. a padded portfolio doesn't survive 20 seconds of it. a genuinely strong one outranks any university on your CV.

that's what uncooked is. a trust layer, not a matching tool.

you prove what you can do once. a video pitch. the best thing you've made or done. 2 honest hours, then a human checks it. no bot rejects you for missing a keyword.

that becomes your passport, and it travels with every application you make. every application through us gets an answer. it's the rule.

and we're pointing people somewhere specific.

we think more young people should be building at startups. it's where you get year 1 responsibility, not year 5. it's where new roles are being created while the old graduate path shrinks.

and there have never been more of them. the UK started 56,615 new tech companies last year alone.

there's also uncooked labs, our community for the people building the physical future. robots, rockets, deep tech. more on that here →

so why do all this?

because opportunity, capital and connection still mostly run on where you went and who you know. the way out has always been to put yourself out there and build something worth seeing. we're making that the way in.

1 million young people into meaningful opportunities by 2030. that's the aim.

the people who get somewhere are the ones who build instead of wait. if that's you, this is for you.

come build your proof.
